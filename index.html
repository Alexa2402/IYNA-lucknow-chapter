Why We Started

Bridging the gap between secondary school & modern neuroscience

Standard high school curricula briefly touch upon nervous systems, yet rarely dive into the frontiers of electrophysiology, neural plasticity, neurological pathologies, and neuroethics.

As an official chapter of the International Youth Neuroscience Association (IYNA), our mission in Lucknow is simple: break academic boundaries early. We introduce middle and high school students to authentic academic literature, scientific reasoning, and collaborative medical research before they ever enter university.

Structured Pathway

Your Journey in the Chapter

Whether you are in grade 8 or 12, our progressive pathway guides you through neurobiology step-by-step.

01

Basics of the Brain

Learn what the brain is made of and how billions of tiny cells (neurons) talk to each other using electricity and chemicals.

02

Senses & Movement

Discover how the brain processes what we see, hear, and feel, and how it sends signals to our muscles so we can move.

03

Health & Diseases

Explore what happens when the brain gets sick. We discuss conditions like Alzheimer's, epilepsy, and anxiety, and how doctors treat them.

04

Thinking Like a Scientist

Learn how to read real scientific papers, ask great research questions, and share your own neuroscience ideas with the community.

For High School Students

No formal background required — just curiosity

You do not need to be at the top of your biology class or have laboratory experience. If you wonder how thoughts form or how memories are stored, you belong here.

  • Attend live, interactive neuroscience workshops
  • Participate in peer-led bi-weekly journal clubs
  • Receive curated study materials for the International Brain Bee
  • Learn how to read PubMed and scientific preprints
  • Network with like-minded students across Lucknow and India
  • Contribute to chapter articles, research briefs, and outreach campaigns

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and participating in the chapter.

Do I need prior neuroscience experience?

Not at all. We start with the basics of neuroanatomy and build up to complex topics. Any curious student from 8th grade through 12th grade is welcome.

Is there any registration or membership fee?

No. IYNA is an open-access, non-profit educational initiative. All resources, workshops, and journal club meetings are 100% free.

Are sessions held online or in person?

We host virtual sessions and workshops regularly, alongside select in-person school visits and workshops in Lucknow.

What is the International Brain Bee?

The International Brain Bee is a premier neuroscience competition for high school students. We provide guidance, study materials, and mock quizzes to help you compete.
