Neuroscience · Research · Community

Bringing the brain beyond the classroom.

IYNA Chapter Lucknow is a student-led community chapter of the International Youth Neuroscience Association — making neuroscience and research accessible to high school students.

📍 Based in Lucknow, India

What we do

🧠

Neuroscience Workshops

Interactive sessions exploring different areas of neuroscience and the brain.

🔬

Research

Finding papers, understanding studies, and developing research questions.

📖

Journal Club

Read and discuss neuroscience research papers with other students.

🐝

Brain Bee Resources

Free study materials to prepare for the International Brain Bee.

🎤

IYNA Guest Lectures

Talks by members of the wider IYNA community.

For Students
