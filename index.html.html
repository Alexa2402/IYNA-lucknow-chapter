Neuroscience for curious students.

IYNA Chapter Lucknow is a student-led chapter of the International Youth Neuroscience Association. We run workshops, research sessions and journal clubs so high school students can explore the brain beyond the classroom.

Why a neuroscience chapter in Lucknow?

Neuroscience is the study of the brain and nervous system: how we think, remember, feel and move, and what happens when things go wrong. It connects biology, psychology, medicine and computing, yet most high school students never get to explore it in class.

IYNA Chapter Lucknow is a student-led community chapter of the International Youth Neuroscience Association (IYNA). We exist to make neuroscience and research more accessible to students in Lucknow, so you can explore the field early, ask real questions and learn how scientists find answers.

Where we are now

Growing our community

We are an active, student-led chapter running workshops, research sessions, journal clubs and Brain Bee preparation for high school students. We are open to new members and to partnerships with schools and organizations, and we are developing a mentorship program that will connect students with people working in neuroscience.

What we do

Hands-on ways to learn how the brain works and how neuroscientists find out.

Neuroscience workshops

Interactive sessions exploring different areas of neuroscience and the brain, from how neurons communicate to memory, sleep and perception. No prior experience needed.

Research sessions

Learn what scientific research looks like in practice: how to find papers, understand how a study was designed and develop your own research questions.

Journal club

Read a real neuroscience paper, then discuss it with other students. You practice reading critically and explaining science in your own words.

Brain Bee resources

Free study materials to help you prepare for the International Brain Bee, a neuroscience competition for high school students.

IYNA guest lectures

Hear directly from people in the wider IYNA community who work in neuroscience.
